# Borat - It's Very Nice!
---
Released in 2006, **_Borat: Cultural Learnings of America for Make Benefit Glorious Nation of Kazakhstan_** (or simply *Borat*) is a mockumentary comedy film starring Sacha Baron Cohen as the fictional Kazakh journalist Borat Sagdiyev. The film follows Borat’s journey across the United States as he attempts to learn about American culture and customs, often through absurd and provocative encounters with real people.

Blending scripted scenes with unscripted interactions, the film satirizes cultural stereotypes, xenophobia, and American idiosyncrasies. Its bold humor and controversial approach sparked both acclaim and criticism, earning it a cult following and an Academy Award nomination for Best Adapted Screenplay. Borat’s catchphrases and outrageous antics such as 
> It's very nice

or

> Great Success

became pop culture staples, cementing the film’s legacy as one of the most daring comedies of its time.
